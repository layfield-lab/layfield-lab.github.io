Research

What we work on

Five areas, one approach: build careful computational models, test them against experiment where we can, and share the tools.

Peptide folding & enhanced sampling

Short peptides that fold into β-hairpins are simple scaffolds for designing catalysts. Whether they fold depends heavily on the two residues at the turn. We compute the free-energy landscapes of these turns systematically, residue by residue, and use the results to test long-standing design rules.

β-Hairpin turn control in tetrapeptides

A survey of Val-Xaa-Xaa-Leu tetrapeptides comparing D-proline and L-proline turns across all 20 residues, with replicate simulations and validation against a tryptophan-zipper hairpin with experimental data.

Methods
Molecular dynamics; OPES metadynamics and multithermal sampling; machine-learned collective variables (Deep-TICA)
Tools
GROMACS, PLUMED, mlcolvar, MDAnalysis, fes-explore
Earlier work
β-hairpin folding in decapeptides and histidine-containing peptide catalysts (2020–2022)

Biochar & PFAS

Biochar, a charcoal-like material made from organic waste, can remove PFAS (“forever chemicals”) from water, but how well it works varies widely. We build molecular models of biochar from measurable properties and simulate how PFAS bind, to separate the effects of pore size, surface chemistry and water chemistry.

Biochar-Simulator

Generates biochar structures from carbon count, H/C and O/C ratios, defects, functional groups and pore geometry, with force-field files ready for GROMACS.

PFAS adsorption on biochar

Molecular dynamics of PFOA, PFOS and PFBS on biochar surfaces made at different production temperatures.

Methods
Molecular dynamics; enhanced sampling; surface modeling
Tools
GROMACS, RDKit, biochar, biochar-pfas, gromacs-mcp

Catalysis & degradable polymers

Thionolactones open a route to plastics that can break down. We use density functional theory to map how aluminum, yttrium and bismuth catalysts open caprolactone and thionocaprolactone rings, and why some catalysts work better than others. This work is a collaboration with colleagues in St. Thomas Chemistry.

Methods
Density functional theory; transition-state searches; intrinsic reaction coordinates
Tools
Gaussian, pes-builder

Spectroscopy from first principles

Mössbauer-DFT

Mössbauer spectroscopy is a key probe of iron oxidation and spin state. Mössbauer-DFT plans, runs and analyzes ORCA calculations and predicts ⁵⁷Fe isomer shifts and quadrupole splittings against a calibration set, with prediction intervals instead of single numbers.

Rashba splitting in CsPbBr₃

Spin-orbit coupling splits the electronic bands at perovskite surfaces. We are testing whether surface ligands control that splitting on all-inorganic CsPbBr₃.

Methods
Density functional theory; calibration and uncertainty estimates; spin-orbit band structures
Tools
ORCA, Quantum ESPRESSO, PyProcar, Mössbauer-DFT

Interfaces & phase transfer

Phase-transfer catalysts move reactive ions between water and organic solvents. We study how ions cross that interface and how catalyst structure changes the free energy of transfer.

Methods
Molecular dynamics; empirical valence bond models; interface collective variables
Tools
GROMACS, PLUMED, ion-transfer-cv

Earlier work

  • Astrochemistry: reparametrized semi-empirical methods for the vibrational spectra of large interstellar molecules (NASA-funded, 2017–2020).
  • Asymmetric catalysis: computational screening of organocatalysts for [2,3]-Wittig rearrangements.
  • Vibrational probes: connecting molecular dynamics directly to infrared line shapes, with the Londergan group.
